# Disco Boy 0.2.2

Disco Boy is an optional Catastrophe-native music player for Leaf on the
Miniloong Pocket 1. Install or update it through Pak Rat.

## Changes since 0.2.1

- Skip a Music folder on a second SD card that isn't mounted. On the Miniloong
  Pocket 1 the card's mount folder can exist with no card in the slot, and Disco
  Boy could treat that empty folder as a music source.
- Keep your source settings, so the music comes back when you put the card back
  in.

The update preserves your music files and library content. Disco Boy remains
optional and is not included in Leaf SD release ZIPs.
